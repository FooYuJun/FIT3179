Introduction

How much do we know about marine life?

Australia's oceans support an extraordinary range of marine species. By combining marine wildlife observations with environmental data, this visualisation explores where species are found, how sightings have changed over time, and how Australia's changing marine environment may be affecting these ecosystems.

01 / OBSERVATION HISTORY

Marine observations over time

How does the observation record change across reef fish, turtles, and whales and dolphins?

Each line counts records in the supplied dataset, not the number of animals living in the ocean. The datasets cover different time periods, so a missing year means no record was available in that dataset for that year; it does not prove that the species were absent.

Data: Reef fish, turtle, and Australian whale/dolphin occurrence datasets supplied for this project. Counts were aggregated by calendar year from each dataset's event date.

02 / WHERE LIFE IS RECORDED

Where are Australia's marine species observed?

Where are reef fish, turtles, and whales and dolphins recorded across Australian waters?

Points represent recorded occurrence locations rather than estimates of population size. Point size indicates the number of records at the same location, while colour identifies the marine group. Use the filters to explore different groups and years.

Data: Reef fish, turtle, and Australian whale/dolphin occurrence datasets supplied for this project. Geographic coordinates were aggregated by location, marine group, and year.

03 / REEF-FISH BIODIVERSITY

Which reef-fish families dominate?

How are recorded occurrences distributed across the most frequently recorded reef-fish families?

Reef Fish Families

Each segment represents a reef-fish family among the 15 families with the highest number of recorded occurrences.

Longer bars indicate more recorded occurrences. Hover over a segment to explore its record count and reported abundance.

More records do not necessarily mean more individual fish in the ocean. The results reflect the supplied dataset.

Data: Reef-fish occurrences in Australia waters throughout 2006-2026. Records were grouped by fish family, and the 15 families with the most recorded occurrences were selected for comparison.

04 / FISH DIVERSITY OVER TIME

How does fish diversity change over time?

How does the number of distinct reef-fish species recorded within each fish family change across the years?

Each cell shows the number of distinct reef-fish species recorded within a family during a particular year. Darker cells indicate greater observed species diversity. Labridae recorded the highest number of distinct fish species (48 species) across the years, as shown by the darker blue cells in the heatmap.

Data: Reef-fish occurrences in Australia waters throughout 2006-2026. Records were grouped by fish family and calendar year, with the 20 families having the highest overall recorded species diversity selected for comparison.

05 / TURTLE NESTING SEASONALITY

When are turtle nesting observations concentrated?

Which months show the greatest concentration of recorded turtle nesting observations, and does this pattern remain consistent across years?

Turtle Breeding Patterns

Each data point represents the number of turtle observations recorded as "Nesting" for a particular month and year. The lines connect monthly observations to highlight changes in nesting activity over time.

Comparing the yearly patterns reveals differences in the timing and number of recorded nesting observations. Year 1 contains observations for November and December, while the other years show varying patterns, including periods of higher activity and noticeable declines.

These records indicate when nesting was observed in Australia throughout (2020-2024). Differences between years may also reflect variations in data collection and reporting.

Each point shows the number of turtle observations explicitly recorded as "Nesting" during a particular month and year. Higher points indicate more recorded nesting observations, while lower points indicate fewer recorded observations.

Data: Turtle nesting sightings throught 2020-2024. Records labelled "Nesting" in the life stage field were grouped by calendar year and month.

06 / TURTLE OBSERVATION

How does turtle observation vary?

How do monthly turtle observation patterns differ across years, and which months show relatively higher observation activity?

Turtle Observation Patterns

Each horizontal line represents the total turtle observations recorded in a particular month across all available years. The coloured dots indicate the number of recorded observations for each month.

Dots further to the right indicate higher observation counts, helping identify months with the most and fewest recorded sightings and reveal overall seasonal patterns.

These totals combine observations across years (2018-2024) and do not show year-to-year changes.

The horizontal axis shows the total number of turtle observations, while the vertical axis lists the months from January to December. Longer lines and dots further to the right indicate higher observation counts.

Data: Turtle observations were aggregated by month across all available years (2018-2024) to compare overall seasonal patterns.
