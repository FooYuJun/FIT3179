Introduction

How much do we know about marine life?

Australia's oceans support an extraordinary range of marine species. By combining marine wildlife observations with environmental data, this visualisation explores where species are found, how sightings have changed over time, and how Australia's changing marine environment may be affecting these ecosystems.

01 / OBSERVATION HISTORY

Marine observations over time

How does the observation record change across reef fish, turtles, and whales and dolphins?

Each line counts records in the supplied dataset, not the number of animals living in the ocean. The datasets cover different time periods, so a missing year means no record was available in that dataset for that year; it does not prove that the species were absent.

Data: Reef fish, turtle, and Australian whale/dolphin occurrence datasets supplied for this project. Counts were aggregated by calendar year from each dataset's event date.
